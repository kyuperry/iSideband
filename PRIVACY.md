# iSideband Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 29, 2026

iSideband (the “App”) is developed by Kyle Perry (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information iSideband processes, why it is used, and your choices.

## Information We Process

iSideband is designed to operate without an account or centralized messaging server. Depending on the features you use, the App may process:

- Reticulum identity keys and identity hashes generated or imported by you.
- Messages, files, photos, voice recordings, and related metadata that you create or receive.
- Bluetooth device information needed to connect to compatible RNode hardware.
- Wi-Fi/LAN and Raspberry Pi connection details that you enter, such as local hostnames, IP addresses, and ports.
- Network announcements, peer identifiers, routing information, and interface status.
- Location information, if you enable location sharing or use geographic map features.
- Microphone audio, if you enable voice calling or audio recording.

## How Information Is Used

We use this information to establish and secure Reticulum communications; send and receive messages, files, photos, and voice data; connect to Bluetooth RNodes, Wi-Fi/LAN interfaces, and Raspberry Pi or Wi-Fi HaLow interfaces; display network peers, announcements, routes, and maps; provide voice-call and audio features; maintain local settings and message history; and diagnose technical problems when diagnostic information is provided by you.

## Local and Peer-to-Peer Operation

The App is primarily peer-to-peer. Communications may be transmitted over Reticulum networks to intended peers and through network infrastructure required to route them. We do not operate a centralized messaging service for iSideband communications. Reticulum communications use encryption provided by the Reticulum protocol.

## Location Information

Location features are optional. If you grant location permission or share a location through the App, location data may be included in messages or displayed on maps. You can disable location permission in iOS Settings or turn off location sharing within the App.

## Microphone and Audio

Microphone access is used only when you choose an audio-recording or voice-calling feature. Audio may be encoded and transmitted to the peer participating in the communication. You can revoke microphone access at any time in iOS Settings.

## Data Storage and Deletion

The App may store identities, settings, messages, attachments, peer information, and announcements locally on your device. You can delete this information using available controls or by deleting the App. Deleting the App may not delete copies of communications already received by another peer or stored in a device backup.

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. The App may transmit information to communication peers you choose, across Reticulum interfaces needed to deliver communications, or when required by law or necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the App.

## Tracking

iSideband does not use information to track users across apps or websites.

## Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the App’s design to protect locally stored information and communications. No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children’s Privacy

iSideband is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s functionality or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
